## Direction contract

The product is a calm, precise editor's workspace for source material. Its
first job is to make the next source worth reading unmistakable; its second is
to keep the capture and background-work state visible without turning the
whole product into a noisy dashboard.

The black navigation rail is persistent orientation. The warm paper workspace
is a reading surface. Signal orange is reserved for an Owner decision or a
source that needs attention; it is not a generic brand accent. Formal
knowledge stays visually quieter than source evidence, and candidate content
is always labelled as not yet accepted.
