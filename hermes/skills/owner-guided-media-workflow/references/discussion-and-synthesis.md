# Discussion and synthesis

Choose the scope from the Owner's question: single source, related sources, Topic review or Project decision. Read only relevant current records and decisions. Do not force a one-post-at-a-time sequence.

Open with enough dewatered evidence to understand the issue, a compact provenance/gap explanation, and the important increment or disagreement. Then ask the one question most likely to advance credibility judgment, investment choice or synthesis. Do not start ordinary discussion with administrative classification, generic "有什麼想法", or a quiz detached from a real decision.

Offer competing explanations with conditions and evidence. Let the Owner react before presenting a finished personal stance when anchoring would displace their reasoning. Follow the answer rather than a fixed interrogation checklist. Do not manufacture disagreement, insights or learning notes. This workflow supports deliberate thinking; do not claim measured neurological improvement.

Prepare an update containing:
- the Owner's actual position, conditions and reason;
- linked supporting and contrary sources;
- distinctions between Owner words and new AI additions;
- unresolved questions and proposed verification;
- the relevant knowledge note, Topic or Project destination.

Record an explicit acceptance once for its exact scope through the supported authenticated promotion path. Preserve original decision evidence and expected version. If the Owner's words are ambiguous, ask only about the ambiguity. No semantic acceptance may be inferred from a source being collected or a draft being approved.

Accepted understanding remains provisional with respect to truth. Verified status requires observed evidence and test conditions. Preserve earlier versions when the Owner changes their mind. New conflicting evidence produces a revision proposal rather than overwriting a position.

Save each discussion event once with links to involved sources and aggregate records where supported. Until cross-source persistence exists, provide a pending-save synthesis and link existing supported records; do not create shadow authoritative files or claim a new knowledge-note table exists.

Valid outcomes include reusable understanding, explicit uncertainty, reasoned rejection, a small verification proposal or intentional retention. Not every discussion needs a task or creative output. Resume from unresolved questions and prior decisions, not a forced reread of every original post.
